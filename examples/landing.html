03/Principles

Made with care.
Built to last.

Every component follows the same rules, no matter its size.

  1. 01

    Tokens first

    No fixed colors. Everything goes through variables you can change.

  2. 02

    Simple HTML

    Readable BEM classes, easy to turn into Blade components.

  3. 03

    Overridable

    Everything lives in Tailwind layers, so your utilities always win.

  4. 04

    Accessible

    AA contrast, visible focus and full keyboard navigation.

  5. 05

    No dependencies

    Plain, optional JavaScript. No frameworks in the core.

  6. 06

    Subtle motion

    Short animations that switch off with prefers-reduced-motion.
