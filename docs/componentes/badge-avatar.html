Badge and avatar

Counters, status dots and avatars with initials or an image, alone or in a group.

<span class="cb-badge cb-badge--warning">3 días</span>
<span class="cb-badge cb-badge--danger">99+</span>
<span class="cb-badge cb-badge--solid">Pro</span>
<span class="cb-badge cb-badge--dot cb-badge--success" role="img" aria-label="En línea"></span>

Classes and modifiers

ClassDescription
.cb-badgeCounter or short label.
.cb-badge--accent|success|warning|danger|infoSoft tones.
.cb-badge--solidAccent fill.
.cb-badge--dotJust a dot.
.cb-avatar36px avatar. Accepts text or img.
.cb-avatar--sm|lg|xl28, 48 and 64px sizes.
.cb-avatar--square, .cb-avatar--accentSquare shape and accent tone.
.cb-avatar__status--online|busy|awayStatus dot.
.cb-avatar-groupOverlapping avatars.
.cb-user, .cb-user__name, .cb-user__metaAvatar with name and details.

Accessibility

  • A status dot without text needs role="img" and aria-label.
  • If the avatar is an image, its alt should be the person's name.
